How to choose the right page

Match the service to the problem you are seeing.

If you have no hot water, start with the water heater page. If the home has no water or low pressure from a well system, open the well pump page. If the issue is staining, hard water, or water quality, the treatment pages are the best next step. If you smell gas, suspect a regulator problem, need a gas appliance hooked up, or want a gas water heater installed or replaced, open the gas services page.
