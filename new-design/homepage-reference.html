Our Story

How It All Started

Bryant started Afternoon Plumbing while raising his kids as a stay-at-home dad. His days belonged to his family, but when his wife came home each afternoon, he picked up his tools and went to work. The name came naturally, straight from the rhythm of a family making it work.

Bryant sharpened his skills on some of the most demanding jobs in the Baltimore area, apprenticing at the National Aquarium, the Clorox plant, and Oriole Park at Camden Yards. These experiences pushed him to develop a level of precision and problem-solving that carried over into every job he took on.

Over time, word spread and the work grew. Bryant built a reputation for showing up, doing the job right, and treating people fairly.

Today, Bryant and his son Christopher own and operate Afternoon Plumbing together, serving their community with the same straightforward approach that started it all.
