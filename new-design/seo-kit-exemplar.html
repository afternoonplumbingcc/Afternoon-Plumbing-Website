Decide with us, not by guessing

Repair it or replace it

We would rather do the smaller job when the smaller job is right. Here is the honest way we split it.

Repair makes sense when

  • The tank is not leaking and the unit is under about ten years old
  • The problem is a heating element, thermostat, anode rod, gas valve, or pressure relief valve
  • Your venting and fuel setup already fits a standard replacement
  • The repair cost is well clear of the cost of a new unit
Ask us to look at it first

Replacement makes sense when

  • The tank itself is leaking, which cannot be repaired
  • The unit is past ten to twelve years old
  • Repairs are stacking up and the unit keeps failing
  • You want to move to a tankless or heat pump unit for efficiency

Common questions

Water heater questions homeowners ask us

Should I repair my water heater or replace it?

We repair when the tank is not leaking and the unit is under about ten years old. We replace when the tank is leaking, when the unit is past ten to twelve years, or when the repair cost gets close to the cost of a new unit. We look at it and tell you which one we would do in your home.

Can you replace a leaking water heater the same day?

Often yes, depending on availability and what equipment the job needs. Call or text us and we will tell you the soonest we can get there.

Is a tankless water heater better than a tank unit?

Tank units are simpler to install and work with gas or electric fuel. Tankless units heat on demand, save floor space, and run efficiently, but they need more careful venting and gas line sizing. We check your install location before we recommend either one.

Are heat pump water heaters worth it in Maryland?

Often yes. Heat pump units use electricity to move heat instead of creating it, so they run far less electricity than standard electric tanks, especially in conditioned basements and utility rooms. Rebate eligible models can be ordered directly through BGE.

Do I need a permit for a water heater replacement?

Most standard water heater replacements do not need a permit. For larger projects we pull the plumbing rough in permit and schedule inspections, and we tell you about it during the estimate instead of after the work starts.

What does the free estimate cover?

We look at the install location, fuel type, venting, and code requirements, then give you a firm quote in writing before any work starts. If we find something extra once the old unit is removed, we stop and price it with you first. That is where no hidden fees comes from.

How long does a water heater last?

Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years. Tankless units often last 15 to 20 years with proper maintenance. Hard water and skipped flushing can shorten the lifespan.

How often should a water heater be flushed?

Once a year for most homes in Carroll County. Flushing clears the sediment that settles on the tank floor, and that sediment is what makes a tank fail early. We do it as part of a flush and anode service.

What does the anode rod do?

The anode rod is the sacrificial metal piece inside your tank that corrodes instead of the tank itself. When it is eaten away, the tank starts corroding. We check it during service and replace it when it needs replacing.

What size water heater does my household need?

It comes down to how many people are in the house and how often you run hot water at the same time. Most homes our size run fine on a standard 40 to 50 gallon tank. We check your household and existing unit, then size it with you before anything is ordered.

What does the 1 year labor warranty cover?

It covers our labor on the work we did for one year after the job is finished. Equipment carries the warranty from the manufacturer. If something we installed fails in that window, we come back and fix it at no charge for labor.

Do you handle emergency water heater calls?

We do, and leaking tanks are the calls we treat as urgent. We will get out as fast as we can and tell you the soonest we can be there when you call.
