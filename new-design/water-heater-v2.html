When to call us

Call when you notice any of this

Water pooling under the tank

A puddle around the base means the tank is leaking. Shut the water off at the valve and call us the same day.

Hot water runs out fast

Showers go cold after a few minutes. On a tank that usually means sediment, a failing element, or a unit at the end of its life.

The unit is old

Most tanks last 8 to 12 years. Past that point a replacement usually costs less than the repairs you are about to need.

Rumbling and popping

Heavy sediment noise means the tank needs flushing or replacing, and it is worth handling before it settles into the heating elements.

Rust or a rotten egg smell

Orange water or a sulfur smell from hot taps points at corrosion inside the tank. Treat it as a replacement job.

Tripped breaker or dead pilot

No hot water at all on an electric unit means a tripped breaker or failed element. On a gas unit it usually means the pilot will not stay lit.

Not sure which one you have? Send a photo through the contact form or text us. We will tell you whether a repair or a replacement is the sensible call before you spend anything.

Choosing a heater

The four setups we install most

01

Traditional tank

The most common choice for Carroll County homes. Gas or electric fuel, 40 to 80 gallons, with atmospheric or power venting for basements and rooms without a chimney. Simple to install and easy to service.

02

Tankless

Heats water on demand, so it never runs out and it frees up space in a utility room. Venting and gas line sizing decide whether a home can support one, so we confirm that during the estimate. See our heat pump water heater page for the high efficiency option.

03

Gas and propane

Gas heats faster and costs less to run than electric. We install natural gas and propane tanks, including switching a home from electric to gas. Details on our gas water heater installation page.

04

Heat pump

Uses electricity to move heat rather than creating it, so it runs far less electricity than a standard electric tank. Works best in conditioned or partly conditioned basements. We install and plumb the condensate line to a pump where the home needs one.

Common questions

Water heater questions, answered plainly

How long does a water heater last?

Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years. Tankless units often last 15 to 20 years with proper maintenance. Hard water and skipped flushing can shorten the lifespan.

Should I repair or replace my water heater?

If the unit is newer and the tank is not leaking, repair can still make sense. If the tank itself is leaking, replacement is usually the better option. We will look at it and tell you which one we would do in your home.

Can you replace a leaking water heater the same day?

Often yes, depending on availability and what equipment the job needs. Call or text us and we will tell you the soonest we can get there.

Tank or tankless: which is better for my home?

Tank units are simpler to install and work with gas or electric fuel. Tankless units heat on demand, save floor space, and run efficiently, but they need more careful venting and gas line sizing. We check your install location before recommending one.

Do I need a permit for a water heater replacement?

Most standard water heater replacements do not need a permit. For larger projects we pull the plumbing rough-in permit and schedule inspections, and we tell you about it during the estimate rather than after the work starts.

Are heat pump water heaters worth it in Maryland?

Often yes. Heat pump units use electricity to move heat instead of creating it, so they run far less electricity than standard electric tanks, especially in conditioned basements and utility rooms. Rebate-eligible models can be ordered directly through BGE.

What does the free estimate cover?

We look at the install location, fuel type, venting, and code requirements, then give you a firm quote in writing before any work starts. If we find something extra once the old unit is removed, we stop and price it with you first. That is where no hidden fees comes from.
