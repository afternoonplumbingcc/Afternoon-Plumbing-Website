When to call

Signs it is time to replace your gas water heater

  • Age: most gas tanks last 10 to 12 years before failures stack up
  • Rust-colored hot water coming from the taps
  • Rusty water only on the hot side, with a clear cold side
  • Rattling or popping from the burner as sediment hardens on the tank floor
  • A pilot light that keeps going out for no clear reason
  • Water pooling at the base of the tank
  • Gas bills creeping up with the same hot water usage
  • Visible corrosion on the burner compartment or fittings
Gas vs electric

Both make hot water. The best pick depends on the house.

Why Carroll County homeowners pick gas

A gas tank reheats far faster than an electric one, so a busy household is less likely to run out mid-shower. On natural gas, running costs are usually lower than electric; propane pricing varies more, and we will tell you where your fuel lands during the estimate. And when the power is out, a gas tank with a standing pilot keeps making hot water while an electric tank just sits there; electronic-ignition and power-vent models need power, and that is the version we usually recommend when there is no usable chimney.

When electric still makes sense

If the home has no existing gas line or venting path, adding both just to run a water heater is a bigger project than many people want. All-electric homes, small loads like a cottage or an addition, and homes without combustion air can all be better served by electric. If that is your situation, we will say so during the estimate.

Straight talk

What gas water heater jobs look like around Carroll County

Most homes here are already set up for it

A lot of Carroll County homes already have gas service for a furnace, range, or dryer, so replacing a gas water heater is usually a tank-for-tank swap in the same footprint. That is the most common job we see: same spot, same fuel, new tank, and hot water back the same day.

When the chimney is not an option

Not every home has a usable chimney or venting path where the old tank sat. In those cases we look at power venting instead, which sends exhaust out through a side wall, or a tankless gas water heater, which wall-mounts, vents on its own path, and never runs out of hot water - the gas line sizing gets quoted as part of the same estimate. We weigh it against what the house allows and talk through the choice during the estimate, before anything is scheduled.

Common questions

Gas water heater FAQ

How long does a gas water heater installation take?

Most tank-for-tank gas water heater replacements take about half a day, including removal of the old tank, the gas connection, leak testing, and a full fill and fire. Jobs that also need a new gas line or new venting can take longer, and we tell you that upfront when we quote.

Do you remove the old tank?

Yes. Removal and haul-away of the old tank is part of our standard gas water heater replacement. We drain and disconnect it safely, get it out of your home without damaging floors or walls, and take it with us for proper disposal.

Gas vs electric water heater: which is better for Westminster homes?

If your home already has gas service for a furnace, range, or dryer, a gas water heater usually heats water faster, and on natural gas it usually costs less to run (propane pricing varies more), and it keeps working through power outages if it has a standing pilot (electronic-ignition and power-vent models need power). Electric makes sense when there is no existing gas line or venting or in an all-electric home. We do the fuel and venting check during the free estimate and tell you honestly which way makes sense.

Can you switch me from electric to gas?

Yes. Switching a home from an electric water heater to a gas one means running a gas line from the meter or tank, adding proper venting, and confirming combustion air. We quote the entire path, not just the tank, so you get one clear number before any work starts.

What size gas water heater do I need?

The right size depends on the number of people in the home, bathroom count, and whether you run a large tub, dishwasher, or washer at the same time. We confirm the first hour rating and recovery during the estimate. Most Westminster homes land on a common stocked tank size that fits the existing footprint.

Why is my hot water rusty?

Rust that shows up only on the hot side usually means the inside of the tank is corroding, often because the anode rod has been spent for a while. Rust on the cold side can point to galvanized supply piping or the well or municipal supply instead. A tank that is rusting internally is near the end of its life and should be evaluated for replacement.

How much does gas water heater replacement cost?

Every install is different. The price depends on the tank size and efficiency, the condition of the venting, the gas connection, and whether the new tank fits the existing footprint. We inspect all of it and give you a free, upfront quote before any work starts, with no hidden fees.
