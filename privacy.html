Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Afternoon Plumbing collects through afternoonplumbing.com, how it is used, and the choices available to you. It is written in plain language on purpose.

Questions about this policy?

Call or text 443-789-2897 or email afternoonplumbingcc@gmail.com.

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Information we collect

Contact information you choose to send us. When you call, text, or email, you may share your name, phone number, email address, service address, and descriptions or photos of the plumbing issue. This is used to respond to your request, prepare estimates, and schedule and perform work.

Job information. For work performed, we keep records of the service, materials, and pricing associated with the job for accounting and warranty purposes.

Photos of work. With permission, before and after photos may be kept for marketing purposes. Photos avoid house numbers, faces, mail, and street names.

How information is used

Information is used to provide plumbing services, communicate about estimates and scheduled work, and keep business records. Basic, aggregated information may be used to understand which services and pages are most helpful.

When information is shared

We do not sell customer information. Information is shared only when needed to perform work, or when required by law.

Website and analytics

This website is hosted on Google Cloud. Like most websites, the hosting platform may automatically collect standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This information is not used to identify individual customers.

Data retention

Business and service records are kept as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy and to meet legal or accounting obligations.

Your choices

To review, correct, or request deletion of your information, call or text 443-789-2897 or email afternoonplumbingcc@gmail.com. Requests are handled promptly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
