How long does a water heater last?

Most tank water heaters last 8-12 years. Tankless units often last 15-20 years with proper maintenance. Hard water and skipped flushing can shorten the lifespan, so tanks on hard well water deserve a yearly check.

Do you handle small jobs like faucets and toilets?

Yes. Everyday plumbing is a big share of the work in Columbia: faucet and toilet repairs and replacements, disposals, shutoff valves, laundry faucets, and hose bibs. Small jobs get the same free estimate as big ones.

Why do hose bibs freeze and burst here?

Water trapped in an outdoor faucet, or in the short pipe behind it, freezes and splits the pipe inside the wall. Frost-free hose bibs drain themselves when shut off. If yours drips, or a hose was left connected through winter, have it checked before the first hard freeze. We cover the whole subject on our hose bib replacement page.

Sometimes, if the scope is clear. More involved jobs may need to be seen in person first so the estimate stays accurate. Either way, the estimate is free before you commit to anything.
