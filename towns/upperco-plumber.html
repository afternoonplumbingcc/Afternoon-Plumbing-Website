How long do well pumps last?

Most well pumps last 8-15 years depending on water quality, usage, pump type, and depth. Short cycling from a waterlogged pressure tank can wear a pump out early, so we test the whole system before recommending a replacement. See our well pump repair page for what a no-water visit covers.

Is the well water here hard on water heaters?

A lot of Baltimore County wells produce hard water, and the scale it drops in the bottom of a tank shortens water heater life and efficiency. A right-sized water softener protects the heater and every fixture downstream.

What causes blue-green stains on my fixtures?

Blue-green stains usually mean acidic water, which is common in Baltimore County wells. Low pH slowly eats copper pipe and causes pinhole leaks. An acid neutralizer sized to your water test stops the corrosion at the source.

What should we winterize before the cold arrives?

Disconnect garden hoses, confirm the hose bibs are frost-free, insulate exposed piping in unheated spaces, and know where the main shutoff sits. A winterization visit checks the water heater, well system, and treatment equipment before the first hard freeze.
