Why do hose bibs freeze and burst here?

Water trapped in an outdoor faucet, or in the short pipe behind it, freezes and splits the pipe inside the wall. Frost-free hose bibs drain themselves when shut off. If yours drips, or a hose was left connected through winter, have it checked before the first hard freeze. We cover the whole subject on our hose bib replacement page.

How long do well pumps last?

Most well pumps last 8-15 years depending on water quality, usage, pump type, and depth. Short cycling from a waterlogged pressure tank can wear a pump out early, so we test the whole system before recommending a replacement. See our well pump repair page for what a no-water visit covers.

Is the well water here hard on water heaters?

A lot of Carroll County wells produce hard water, and the scale it drops in the bottom of a tank shortens water heater life and efficiency. A right-sized water softener protects the heater and every fixture downstream.

Yes. Everyday plumbing is a big share of the work in Hampstead: faucet and toilet repairs and replacements, disposals, shutoff valves, laundry faucets, and hose bibs. Small jobs get the same free estimate as big ones.
