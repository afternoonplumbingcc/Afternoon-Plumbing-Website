Our Story

How It All Started

Bryant started Afternoon Plumbing while raising his kids as a stay-at-home dad. His days belonged to his family, but when his wife came home each afternoon, he picked up his tools and went to work. The name came naturally, straight from the rhythm of a family making it work.

Bryant sharpened his skills on some of the most demanding jobs in the Baltimore area, apprenticing at the National Aquarium, the Clorox plant, and Oriole Park at Camden Yards. These experiences pushed him to develop a level of precision and problem-solving that carried over into every job he took on.

Over time, word spread and the work grew. Bryant built a reputation for showing up, doing the job right, and treating people fairly.

Today, Bryant and his son Christopher own and operate Afternoon Plumbing together, serving their community with the same straightforward approach that started it all.

Services Offered

Q: What plumbing services does Afternoon Plumbing provide?

A: We handle a wide range of residential and commercial plumbing needs, including drain cleaning, leak repairs, pipe installation and replacement, water heater installation and repair, toilet and faucet repairs, garbage disposal service, and sewer line inspections.

Q: Do you work on both residential and commercial properties?

A: Yes! We service both homes and businesses of all sizes.

Q: Do you offer free estimates?

A: Yes, we provide free estimates on most jobs. Contact us to describe your issue and we'll get you a quote.

Pricing & Estimates

Q: How does your pricing work?

A: Our pricing depends on the type of service, materials needed, and the complexity of the job. We provide upfront pricing before any work begins so there are no surprises.

Q: Will I be charged just for someone coming out to look at the problem?

A: We offer free estimates on most services. If a diagnostic visit is required for a complex issue, we'll let you know the cost upfront before scheduling.

Q: Do you accept credit cards and other payment methods?

A: Yes, we accept cash, check, Zelle, and all major credit cards for your convenience.

Scheduling & Availability

Q: How do I schedule an appointment with Afternoon Plumbing?

A: You can call us directly at (443) 789-2897 to book an appointment. We'll work with your schedule to find a convenient time.

Q: How quickly can you come out?

A: We typically offer same-day or next-day appointments for most non-emergency services, depending on availability.

Q: Do you give a heads-up before arriving?

A: Absolutely. We'll call or text you when our plumber is on the way so you're never left waiting around.

Emergency & After-Hours

Q: Do you offer emergency plumbing services?

A: Yes! Plumbing emergencies don't keep business hours, and neither do we. Afternoon Plumbing offers emergency services for urgent issues like burst pipes, major leaks, and sewer backups.

Q: What counts as a plumbing emergency?

A: Common emergencies include burst or frozen pipes, sewage backups, no hot water, overflowing toilets, and significant water leaks that risk property damage.
