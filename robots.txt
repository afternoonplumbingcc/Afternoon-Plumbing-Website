# crawlers are also allowed (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot,

# Applebot, meta-externalfetcher, MistralAI-User, DuckAssistBot).
#
# The token names above are listed as COMMENTS only. They are deliberately NOT
# active "User-agent:" groups. Under RFC 9309 sec 2.2.1 a crawler matches only
# its most specific group, and groups are never merged with the "*" wildcard
# group, so adding per-bot groups would each need their own
# "Disallow: /new-design/" line. Keeping the policy as comments means the
# design sandbox below can only ever be governed by this one wildcard group,
# which is the simplest and safest form of the file.
#
