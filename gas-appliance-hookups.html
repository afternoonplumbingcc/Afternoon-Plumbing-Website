How to choose the right page

Match the service to the problem you are seeing.

If you smell gas or suspect a regulator problem, start with the regulator page. If your issue is an appliance hookup or a gas line repair, call us and we will point you to the right next step.

  • Sulfur or rotten-egg smell near the regulator or gas line
  • Visible corrosion, frost, or soot on the regulator body
  • Appliances that flame yellow, pop, or go out unexpectedly
  • Need to add a new gas appliance to an existing gas line
Safety first

If you smell gas, do not wait on us.

Active gas leaks are an emergency. Leave the home, do not flip switches, and call your gas utility or 911 from a safe distance. Once the utility clears the scene, call Afternoon Plumbing for the house-side repair. Our gas leak safety guide walks through the four emergency steps and what to do while you wait for the utility.

Utility numbers: BGE: 1-800-685-0123. Washington Gas: 1-844-927-4427. Columbia Gas: 1-888-460-4332. For propane suppliers, the number is on your tank or your billing statement.
